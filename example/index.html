lightweight-magic-charts

A host's mount: the library composes, lays out, labels and keyboard-reaches; this page supplies the vocabulary and the numbers. No backend, no credential, and the candles come from a fixed seed, so every load draws the same chart. Open Studies to add the moving averages over the price or the strength ratio in a lane of its own — all three are computed here, not by the library, which owns no arithmetic and no vocabulary. The notice about the history-to-live seam is the library telling the truth rather than failing: this port has no live channel, so the seam cannot be proven.
